# ReplyWell Staff Walkthrough

## Purpose

ReplyWell turns a customer complaint into a calm, professional draft reply. It is a writing assistant, not an automatic responder.

## Before you start

- Open the ReplyWell page: https://replywell.onrender.com
- Make sure you have an internet connection.
- Have the customer's complaint ready.
- Do not enter passwords, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

## Generate a reply

1. Paste the customer's message into **What did your customer say?**
2. The character counter will show how much of the 4,000-character limit you have used.
3. Select **Generate reply**.
4. Wait while the button shows **Writing your reply...**
5. Read the draft in the **Your draft** section.

## Review before sending

Before sending, confirm that the draft:

- Names the specific issue accurately
- Uses the correct customer name or order/booking reference
- Makes no unsupported refund, compensation, deadline, or resolution promise
- Includes only facts your business can confirm
- Uses the correct tone for the customer and situation

Edit the draft if needed. If the issue is missing important details, send a follow-up request for the order, booking, tracking, or contact information needed to investigate.

## Copy the reply

1. Select **Copy reply** below the draft.
2. Paste the text into WhatsApp, email, or your customer-support system.
3. Add or change any business-specific wording.

If clipboard access is blocked, select the draft text manually and copy it.

## If an error appears

- **Enter a complaint message first:** Add a customer complaint before submitting.
- **Under 4,000 characters:** Remove text until the complaint fits.
- **Reply service is temporarily unavailable:** Wait briefly and try again.
- **Reply generation is not configured:** This is an administrator/API-key issue; contact the app owner.
- **Unexpected response:** Refresh the page and try again.

## Responsible use

- Treat every draft as a starting point.
- Do not send generated text without human review.
- Do not add claims or promises the business cannot keep.
- Remove sensitive customer information after the issue is resolved.
- Escalate threats, harassment, discrimination, fraud, or safety concerns to the business owner or designated support lead.
